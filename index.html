Trips Concept Prototype
Try it yourself · 60 seconds

Know the manners before you land

A concept for Duolingo that turns a travel date into a plan, keeps it useful in the country, and carries the habit home. Switch between Free and Trip Pass at the top of the phone to see what each one gets.

FREETRIP PASSSUPER
  1. Before: pick Japan or Korea and your exact travel date
  2. Take the 60-second trip-ready check (5 situations)
  3. See how locals react and how serious each slip is
  4. Earn your badge and share it
  5. In the country: phrases, polite or casual, and regional dialects
  6. Back home: Trip Wrapped and the Super offer

Built on learning research

  • Guess first. Trying before being taught improves later recall (Richland, Kornell and Kao, 2009).
  • Severity ratings show which slips matter most, and the most serious mistakes come first in review.
  • Beginner and intermediate paths, so lessons start at the right level.

Concept prototype by SVR Consulting for the think-cell Global Case Competition 2026. Illustrative only, not an official Duolingo product. No sign-in and no payment. Calls and speech scoring are simulated. Japan etiquette draws on the Japan Tourism Agency's "Travel Etiquette for the Future" (Nov 2024) and travel etiquette guides.
